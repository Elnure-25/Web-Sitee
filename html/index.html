About Us

A Lakeside Hotel
in the Mountains

Montana Resort sits on the quiet north shore of Lake Arrow,
forty minutes from the nearest town and five from the first
trailhead. Thirty-two rooms and suites, a restaurant that
cooks with what the valley grows, a small spa, and a jetty
where the day starts with coffee and ends with the light
going pink on the peaks.
